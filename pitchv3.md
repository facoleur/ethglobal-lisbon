# TAR

---

You lost your phone.

Your wallet is gone. Every token, every asset, everything — locked behind a device you'll never hold again.

This is the moment the whole industry has been trying to solve.

---

The old answer was a list of twelve words. Write them down. Hide them. Never lose them. Never show them to anyone. Memorize them. Store them somewhere fireproof. Laminate them. Split them in half and bury them in two different places.

Nobody does this. Of course nobody does this.

---

So the new answer arrived: just use your Google account. Your iCloud. Let us hold it for you.

And it worked. Millions of people finally started using wallets.

But here's what actually happened.

You handed the keys to your financial life to a company. A company with lawyers. A company that receives government requests. A company that gets hacked. A company that can lock your account, lose your data, or simply change its terms of service one Tuesday morning.

You didn't solve the problem. You just made it someone else's problem — and hoped they'd be nice about it.

**You went from being your own bank to trusting a bank you've never heard of.**

---

What if there was a third way?

No list of words. No company holding your keys. No one you have to trust.

Just math. And human nature.

---

## The bet

Here's the insight at the heart of TAR.

Losing your wallet is a secret. Only you know you lost it. The rest of the world has no idea.

So what if recovering your account required putting money on the line first?

Anyone who wants to take over your account must publicly commit a sum of money — and then wait. Days. Weeks. Whatever window you choose.

During that time, if you're still around, you see the attempt. You cancel it. They lose their money. Attack over.

But if you genuinely lost everything — you're the one who initiates the recovery. You wait out the window. Nobody else knows your account is truly gone, so nobody else takes the risk of challenging you.

The thief loses. The real owner wins. Every time.

**Not because we trust you. Because we built a system where betting against you is a terrible idea.**

---

## The bodyguard who can't rob you

You might want a second layer. A friend, a family member — someone who watches over your account and can block a suspicious recovery attempt.

In every wallet that exists today, giving someone that power means giving them the power to take everything from you.

Not here.

In TAR, your guardians can do exactly one thing: say no. They cannot touch your money. They cannot move a single cent. They are a lock, not a key.

For the first time, you can ask someone to protect you without putting yourself at their mercy.

---

## The bluff that protects everyone

Now here's the part that keeps us up at night — in the best way.

What if you don't have any guardian? What if you're alone, and you know it?

It doesn't matter.

Because the attacker doesn't know it.

Some accounts have guardians watching. Some don't. From the outside, they all look the same. Every attack carries the same risk: commit your money, wait — and maybe lose it all to a guardian you never saw coming.

That uncertainty is the shield.

TAR doesn't just protect people who set up guardians. **It wraps every single account in the doubt of whether a guardian exists.** The system protects you even if you never told a single person about it.

---

No words to memorize.  
No company to trust.  
No guardian who can betray you.  
No way for an attacker to know if you're defenseless.

Just a bet nobody wants to make against you.

---

*TAR. Lose your phone. Keep your life.*
