# TAR — Concept Slides
*~2 min spoken*

---

## S1 — Le problème

**Visual:** Photo plein écran — téléphone cassé sur du bitume. Rien d'autre.

**Spoken (~10s):**
> "You lost your phone. Your wallet is gone. Every asset. Gone."

---

## S2 — L'ancienne réponse

**Visual:** Image d'une seed phrase écrite à la main sur un bout de papier froissé. Caption :

> *"where did I put it?"*

**Spoken (~10s):**
> "The old answer: memorize twelve words. Nobody does this."

---

## S3 — La nouvelle réponse

**Visual:** Logos iCloud + Google Drive. Sourire mesquin. Caption :

> *"Let us hold it for you."*

**Spoken (~20s):**
> "So the industry said: let us hold it for you.
> And you went from being your own bank — to trusting a bank you've never heard of.
> Subject to hacks. Court orders. Terms of service."

---

## S4 — Le concept TAR

**Visual:** Schéma ultra-simple. Trois lignes :

```
Anyone can attempt recovery.
But it costs money — and time.
If you're still there → you cancel → they lose everything.
```

**Spoken (~30s):**
> "TAR makes recovery open to anyone. But not free.
> To attempt it, you lock money on the line — and wait.
> If you're still around, you cancel it. They lose their stake.
> If you genuinely lost your account — you're the only one who knows.
> So you recover safely. Nobody can beat you to it."

---

## S5 — Le guardian qui ne peut pas te voler

**Visual:** Deux colonnes.

```
Traditional social recovery     TAR watch tower
────────────────────────────    ───────────────
Can ACCESS your account    →    Can only say NO
Can move your funds        →    Cannot touch anything
You need to trust them     →    Trust doesn't matter
```

**Spoken (~20s):**
> "You can add a watch tower — a friend, anyone.
> They can do one thing: block a suspicious recovery.
> They cannot touch your funds. They are a lock, not a key."

---

## S6 — Le bluff

**Visual:** Rangée de comptes identiques. Certains avec une icône de watch tower cachée, d'autres non. Impossible de distinguer lesquels.

> *Who has a guardian?*
> *Nobody knows.*

**Spoken (~25s):**
> "From the outside, every account looks the same.
> Some have watch towers. Some don't. The attacker can never know.
> The uncertainty itself is the shield.
> TAR protects you — even if you set up nothing at all."

---
