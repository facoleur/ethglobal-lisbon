Meet TAR 

You can recover your account at anytime. Actually anyone can try to recover YOUR account. But it is not free: you need to lock an amount for a given duration.

If you try to access an account and the owner notice it during the lock time window, you lost your stake.

But if YOU lost your account, you are the only one to know. so you can safely recover it.

But what if someone knows that you lost accses to your account, or can frontrun you in the recovery mechanism?

TAR solves this with social recovery actors, that can not access your accout, but only refuse someone to access your account.
